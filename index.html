My vision

The next leap is agents that act, not just answer.

Chatbots retrieve and respond. Agents plan, use tools, check their own work, and get things done. I'm building toward agentic systems that are safe enough for banks and hospitals: grounded in real data, observable at every step, and accountable for every action.
